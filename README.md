# velo_roads_analysis

Data analysis of bike lanes and bike parkings in Baku, based on open data.

- Calculated the total length of existing bike lanes and found the street with the longest lanes
- Identified "lonely" parkings (more than 50 m from any lane) and parkings outside the city frame
- Gave a strategic, data-based recommendation for the next bike lane

## Datasets

- **Bike lanes:** https://opendata.az/@azerbaycan-yerustu-neqliyyat-agentliyi/velosiped-zolaqlarinin-cografi-koordinatlari
- **Bike parkings:** https://opendata.az/@azerbaycan-yerustu-neqliyyat-agentliyi/velosiped-parkinq-menteqelerinin-cografi-koordinatlari
